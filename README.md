## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Features Used

This project was built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**, using:

- 📗 **Microsoft Excel** — dashboard development and data analysis
- 📊 **PivotTables** — summarising and analysing sales data
- 📈 **Excel Charts** — visualising revenue, profit, targets, and performance
- 🎛️ **Slicers & Interactive Filters** — filtering by year, region, category, and order status
- 🎯 **KPI Cards & Conditional Visuals** — highlighting key business metrics and progress
- 🟪 **Heatmap Formatting** — comparing revenue across categories and regions

## 🎯 Project Overview

The **Sales Analytics Dashboard** brings key business metrics together
in one place. Interactive filters allow users to explore performance by
**Year, Region, Category, and Order Status**. Charts and KPI cards
update with the selected filters, making it easier to identify trends,
compare results, and spot areas that need attention.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

Use the dashboard filters to explore the data from different angles:

-   📅 **Year** --- select the reporting year.
-   🌍 **Region** --- compare geographical performance.
-   🏷️ **Category** --- focus on a product category.
-   🚚 **Order Status** --- analyse delivered, shipped, processing, or
    cancelled orders.

## 📊 Visualisations & Analysis

### 1. 📆 Monthly Revenue, Profit & Target

Compares monthly sales revenue and profit against the target to reveal
performance trends over time.

### 2. 👥 Salesperson Target Achievement

Shows each salesperson's target achievement percentage, helping compare
individual performance.

### 3. 🛒 Revenue & Profit by Category

Compares revenue and profit across product categories to highlight the
strongest contributors.

### 4. 🌐 Revenue vs Target by Region

Evaluates regional revenue against target and helps identify high- and
low-performing regions.

### 5. 🏆 Salesperson Revenue vs Target

Compares actual revenue with target for each salesperson.

### 6. 🍩 KPI Progress Charts

Quickly communicates: - Profit margin - Target achievement - Delivered
orders - Shipped orders - Processing orders - Cancelled orders

### 7. 🔝 Top 10 Performance Rankings

Highlights the top 10: - Products by revenue - States by revenue -
Customers by revenue

### 8. 🟪 Revenue Heatmap --- Category × Region

Uses colour intensity to make it easier to compare category revenue
across regions and spot strong or weak combinations.

## 🧭 How to Explore the Dashboard

1.  Open the dashboard.
2.  Select a **Year**, **Region**, **Category**, or **Order Status**.
3.  Review the KPI cards to understand the selected segment's
    performance.
4.  Compare monthly trends, categories, regions, and salespeople.
5.  Use the Top 10 charts and heatmap to explore detailed performance.

## 📁 Dashboard Sections

-   **Dashboard** --- interactive overview and visual analysis.
-   **Pivot Tables** --- supporting summaries and calculations.
-   **Raw Data** --- underlying sales records.

## 📌 Business Value

This dashboard helps users:

-   Monitor sales and profitability in one view.
-   Track performance against targets.
-   Compare categories, regions, products, customers, and salespeople.
-   Understand order-status distribution.
-   Identify trends and opportunities for better business decisions.
